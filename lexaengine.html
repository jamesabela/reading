THE CORE TECHNOLOGY

LexaEngine™

A flexible language system designed to power a wide range of educational applications that rely on structured vocabulary and text understanding.

📚

Levelled Lexical Database

At its core is a large, levelled lexical database, enabling it to recognise how common, complex, or specialised a word is. This allows applications to support learners at different stages, from basic everyday language through to advanced academic vocabulary.

🏗️

Reusable Foundation

Designed as a reusable foundation that can analyse, organise, and transform language into meaningful outputs. It takes raw input such as individual words, lists, or full texts and structures them in ways that support learning.

🚀

Scalable & Extensible

By separating the engine from the application, LexaEngine™ provides a scalable approach, allowing multiple tools—from spelling tools to adaptive learning platforms—to benefit from the same intelligent handling of language.
