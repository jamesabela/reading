Reading Challenges

Some letters such as b, d, p, and q may be harder to tell apart.

Letters within a word may appear in a different order.

Similar-looking words may be confused, such as was and saw.

Short function words like the, and, or to may be skipped.

Inconsistent spacing can make reading less predictable.
