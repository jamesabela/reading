Episode IV

A NEW WORD

It is a period of lexical war. Rebel vocabularies, striking from a hidden base, have won their first victory against the evil Galactic Empire of Monotony.

During the battle, Rebel spies managed to steal secret plans to the Empire's ultimate weapon, the BOREDOM STAR, an armored space station with enough power to destroy an entire interesting paragraph.

Pursued by the Empire's sinister agents, Princess Literacy races home aboard her starship, custodian of the stolen plans that can save her people and restore variety to the galaxy....
